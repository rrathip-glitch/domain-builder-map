- **Data, not interface (orchestrator's list at the bottom of this file):** the portal hides research wording and treats old links as dated by display rules, so the workbook can still disagree with the screen; D.R. Horton has duplicate people, regions and land-banking rows; the May 2024 Domain one-pager is tagged Verified for Lennar and Inferred for five other builders.

### Three things I would change with another hour
1. **Make the data say what the screen says.** Clean the research wording out of titles and gaps, remove the D.R. Horton duplicates and settle the one-pager's confidence, so the workbook, Coverage and the portal agree without display filters in between.
2. **Send a small first payload.** Home and the Builders list need a few hundred kilobytes, not the whole 10 MB map; load each builder's detail when it is opened, so the first visit on hotel or phone-tethered wifi shows numbers in a second.
3. **Test the three things I could only emulate:** print the brief on paper, send one real newsletter to a test inbox, and read the org chart and By state from across a room on a real shared screen.
